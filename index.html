About

I lead engineering teams that ship predictably, and I stay close enough to the code to know what that takes.

I’m based in the Toronto area and currently lead two distributed engineering teams at MitraTech, spanning India, the US, and Mexico. Before that, I managed Sportsbook API engineering at DraftKings and led teams at Staples Canada, LifeWorks, and Ontario Health. I started as a hands-on developer in 2012 and moved into leadership in 2019.

My work sits where people, process, and platforms meet: scaling distributed teams, redesigning SDLC and CI/CD for predictable delivery, migrating monoliths to event-driven microservices on Kafka, and leading engineering-wide adoption of AI development tooling.

I partner with executives on roadmaps, headcount, and capacity planning, and I still lead design discussions, architecture decisions, and code reviews in C#, T-SQL, and TypeScript on Azure.
